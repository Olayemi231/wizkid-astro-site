There have been a lot of rumours recently about Wizkid planning a new world tour, and fans are already trying to guess which cities he might visit. A few promoters have reportedly been in contact with his team, and some venues have hinted at major Afrobeats bookings coming up this year. Nothing has been confirmed yet, but the excitement online is growing every day.

If the tour happens, it will be Wizkid’s first major run since Made in Lagos, which was one of the most successful Afrobeats tours ever. Many fans are hoping he’ll perform in cities he hasn’t visited before, especially in Europe and North America. Whether the rumours are true or not, it’s clear that people are ready to see him back on stage doing what he does best. All eyes are now on his social media for official updates.
